# Bitcoin Investment Calculator

Bitcoin's price history is full of dramatic cycles. This calculator answers a simple question: **what could your money be worth today if you had invested in Bitcoin on an earlier date?**

<BitcoinInvestmentCalculator title="What if you had invested in Bitcoin?" defaultCurrency="usd" locale="en-US" />

## How the calculation works

The tool divides your hypothetical investment by Bitcoin's market price on the selected date. It then multiplies that BTC amount by the latest available price.

**Bitcoin acquired = investment ÷ historical BTC price**

**Estimated value today = Bitcoin acquired × current BTC price**

The result is an estimate. Exchange fees, spreads, custody costs and taxes are not included, and intraday prices may differ from the daily market price used by the data provider.

## Why timing matters with Bitcoin

Bitcoin has experienced strong bull markets as well as deep drawdowns. A starting date can completely change the result, which is why this calculator uses the date you select instead of relying on a single headline return.
