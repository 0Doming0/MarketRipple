## Structural Headaches for Local Banks

The issue with traditional finance no stop for international wire; e dey affect local corner-shops and family savings every single day.

1.  **Massive Unbanked Population:** Data shows say about **36% of the Nigerian adult population** no get any single bank account, meaning they dey completely outside the formal financial system.
2.  **The Collapse of Traditional 'Ajo':** Since standard banking hard to reach, people don use local informal cooperative contributions (*Ajo*) for decades. However, inside urban areas, participation don **drop by 20%** because default rates and institutional collapse don reach **15%**.
3.  **Inside Fraud:** To make matter worse, operational fraud from the managers of these local savings groups dey hit **15% among traditional groups**, wiping out family savings overnight.
